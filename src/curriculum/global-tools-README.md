## Top 10 "Must-Have" Tools for Chinese Families

These are ranked by: China accessibility + K-12 relevance + parent willingness to pay + educational depth.

### 1. ELSA Speak — English Pronunciation AI
**URL:** https://elsaspeak.com  
**Why top 10:** The rare tool that is China-accessible, has Chinese App Store payment (Alipay), has a Chinese UI, and solves a real daily pain (English pronunciation). Chinese families understand and value pronunciation coaching. Pro ~$99.99/yr.  
**原点 angle:** Resell discounted annual plans as bundles; co-brand "ELSA × 原点英语口语训练营"

### 2. Canva Magic Studio (Education)
**URL:** https://www.canva.com  
**Why top 10:** Free for verified K-12 students/teachers, not GFW-blocked, has Chinese interface (canva.cn), accepts Alipay. AI design tools are increasingly essential for student projects and presentations. Zero friction.  
**原点 angle:** Incorporate Canva Pro for Education into platform — already free, use as a hook to attract families; upsell Canva Pro for parents.

### 3. Grammarly (Pro)
**URL:** https://www.grammarly.com  
**Why top 10:** Not GFW-blocked, browser extension works in China, 12M+ Chinese users already, $12/mo is affordable, and English writing skills are P0 for college-bound students. Affiliate program available.  
**原点 angle:** Affiliate referrals for Pro upgrades; bundle with English essay courses; "AI English Writing Coach" module using Grammarly as the backend.

### 4. Photomath
**URL:** https://photomath.com  
**Why top 10:** 300M+ downloads, free camera-based math solver, available on Chinese App Stores, trusted by Chinese middle/high school students for homework. Plus plan ($69.99/yr) adds video explanations.  
**原点 angle:** Recommend as a companion tool; potential partnership for custom Chinese math content integration.

### 5. Microsoft Math Solver
**URL:** https://math.microsoft.com  
**Why top 10:** Completely free, has Chinese language interface, not GFW-blocked, covers K-12 math. Zero barrier to entry. Best free math tool for Chinese families.  
**原点 angle:** Integrate as a free tier recommendation to drive trust; differentiate by offering Chinese curriculum-aligned practice on top.

### 6. Quizlet (with Q-Chat)
**URL:** https://quizlet.com  
**Why top 10:** Not GFW-blocked, Chinese students already use free flashcards heavily, 700M+ study sets including Chinese content, Q-Chat AI tutor in Plus. Affiliate program confirmed.  
**原点 angle:** Affiliate for Plus upgrades; pre-build 原点-branded study sets for Chinese students (gaokao vocab, biology terms, etc.); cross-promote with platform courses.

### 7. Synthesis
**URL:** https://www.synthesis.com  
**Why top 10:** High-concept brand (Elon Musk connection), not GFW-blocked, game-based STEM problem-solving — fills the gap between rote practice and genuine reasoning skills that Chinese parents increasingly recognize they need. $29-45/mo.  
**原点 angle:** Position as the "thinking skills" complement to exam prep; resell family plans at small margin; use as a premium upsell for families who already do well academically.

### 8. Suno (AI Music)
**URL:** https://suno.com  
**Why top 10:** Not GFW-blocked, generous free tier (10 songs/day), generates music in Chinese, viral engagement potential. Students making their own songs as study mnemonics is a genuine educational use case with high shareability.  
**原点 angle:** "AI音乐记忆法" — create study songs using Suno; viral content hook for platform promotion; Pro plan ($8/mo) is affordable for families interested in music education.

### 9. ElevenLabs
**URL:** https://elevenlabs.io  
**Why top 10:** Not GFW-blocked, supports Chinese voice generation, free tier is genuinely useful (10K credits/mo), and increasingly essential for creating educational audio content. Starter plan at $6/mo.  
**原点 angle:** Use internally to produce Chinese-language educational audio; resell Creator plans via affiliate; offer "AI配音课" workshops teaching students to produce their own audio content.

### 10. Codecademy (or Replit AI)
**URL:** https://www.codecademy.com / https://replit.com  
**Why top 10:** Coding skills are a clear priority for Chinese families. Both platforms are accessible from China. Codecademy's structured curriculum ($14.99/mo annual) is more beginner-friendly; Replit's browser IDE removes setup friction. Codecademy has an active affiliate program.  
**原点 angle:** "少儿编程" module powered by Codecademy curriculum; affiliate revenue from Plus sign-ups; position as alternative to 猿辅导/编程猫 with international credentials.
